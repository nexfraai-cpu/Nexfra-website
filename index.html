Services We Provide

Custom Heavy Body Engineering & Manufacturing

Beyond standard catalog models, Nexfra Manufacturing offers complete end-to-end heavy engineering services. We build custom-tailored load bodies to suit any truck chassis brand (Tata, Ashok Leyland, BharatBenz, Volvo, Scania, Eicher, Mahindra).

Steel Grade & Thickness Tailoring

Select between high-tensile ST52 steel and certified Hardox 450 wear plate. Customize floor plates from 4mm up to 10mm and side wall plates from 4mm to 6mm based on payload abrasiveness.

Hydraulics & Axle Suspension Engineering

High-pressure multi-stage front-end and underbody hydraulic tipping cylinders, PTO drive assemblies, tandem or tridem mechanical leaf spring / air suspensions, and heavy 13T/16T axles.

Custom Accessories & Fitment Add-ons

GPS tracking systems, rear safety cameras, hydraulic loading ramps, extra heavy-duty toolboxes, specialized side hooks, marker light packages, and custom corporate polyurethane spray painting.

Automated Fabrication Quality Assurance

100% ARAI, BIS, and Motor Vehicle Rules compliant. Every body undergoes CNC plasma precision cutting (<0.5mm tolerance), robotic submerged arc welding, shot blasting, and ultrasonic testing.

Built to Outperform, Engineered to Endure

At Nexfra, every trailer, tipper, and heavy load body is crafted under a single unyielding mandate: zero structural failure under extreme payload stress.
